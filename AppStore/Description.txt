PikaDex is the ULTIMATE encyclopedia for all things in the Pokémon world! This is the best companion app for playing through all the core games, from Red/Blue/Yellow to Sword/Shield.


All the information you need to succeed:
- Pokémon Info
- Moves
- Items
- Locations
-- Capture methods for each Pokémon
-- Maps for Generation 5 and earlier
- Abilities
- Evolutions
- Types
- Natures
- TMs/HMs/TRs

Dual Type Checker
Check type effectiveness for dual-type Pokémon, and see all the Pokémon with those types!

Battle Assistant
Never before seen in any other app, select the Pokémon you're fighting against and its level, and view compiled tactical information in a streamlined interface. No more looking through multiple pages to figure out how to fight it!

Teams
PikaDex comes with a built-in team builder from the initial release! Keep track of all your teams, specific to each game. Track your Pokémon's stats, moves, natures, held items, and more!

Other Features
- Dark Mode (!!!)
- Search in all major lists
- Filters for Pokémon list
- Dynamic text sizing for improved readability
