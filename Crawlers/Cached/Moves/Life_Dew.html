Life Dew (Japanese: いのちのしずく Life Droplets) is a non-damaging Water-type move introduced in Generation VIII.

Effect

Life Dew restores the HP of the user and its allies by 25% of their maximum HP. If an ally has Water Absorb, Storm Drain, or Dry Skin, it will activate and negate this move's effect on it.

Description

Games Description
SwShBDSP The user scatters mysterious water around and restores the HP of itself and its ally Pokémon in the battle.

Trivia

  • This move originated as an Ace Spec Item card in the TCG.
  • Along with Body Press, Life Dew is one of the only Generation VIII moves to be usable in Brilliant Diamond and Shining Pearl.
