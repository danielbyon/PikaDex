V-create (Japanese: Ｖジェネレート V-generate) is a damage-dealing Fire-type move introduced in Generation V. In Generation VIII, it is one of the signature moves of Victini.

Effect

Generations V to VII

V-create deals damage, then lowers the user's Defense, Special Defense, and Speed stats by one stage each.

Generation VIII

Pokémon Sword and Shield

From Versions 1.0 to 1.2.1 of Pokémon Sword and Shield, V-create could not be selected in a battle. Starting with Version 1.3.0, it is usable and functions the same as it did in previous generations.

Pokémon Brilliant Diamond and Shining Pearl

V-create cannot be selected in a battle.

Trivia

Artwork of Victini using V-create
  • V-create has the highest base power of all Fire-type moves, except Z-Moves.
    • An Inferno Overdrive based on V-create has the highest base power of all Fire-type moves.
  • This move is one of Victini's animations in Pokédex 3D.
  • This is the only unused move in Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Gates to Infinity that was removed from Pokémon Super Mystery Dungeon.
