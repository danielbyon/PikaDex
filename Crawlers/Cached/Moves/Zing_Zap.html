Zing Zap (Japanese: びりびりちくちく Shocking and Prickly) is a damage-dealing Electric-type move introduced in Generation VII. Prior to Generation VIII, it was the signature move of Togedemaru.

Effect

Zing Zap inflicts damage and has a 30% chance of making the target flinch.
