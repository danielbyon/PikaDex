Dazzling Gleam (Japanese: マジカルシャイン Magical Shine) is a damage-dealing Fairy-type move introduced in Generation VI. It was TM99 in Generation VI and Generation VII, and TM32 in Pokémon: Let's Go, Pikachu! and Let's Go, Eevee!. In Generation VIII, it is TR92.
