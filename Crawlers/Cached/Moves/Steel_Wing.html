Steel Wing (Japanese: はがねのつばさ Steel Wing) is a damage-dealing Steel-type move introduced in Generation II. It was TM47 from Generation II to Generation IV. It regained its TM status in Generation VI, albeit as TM51, until Generation VII. It is TM30 in Generation VIII. It was the signature move of Skarmory in Generation V.

In other games

Pokémon Mystery Dungeon series

In Red Rescue Team and Blue Rescue Team, Steel Wing is a move with base power of 14, 10PP and 90% accuracy. The user targets enemy in front, possibly raising their own Defense stat by one level.

In Explorers of Time and Darkness, the move's accuracy is decreased to 88%.

In Explorers of Sky, the move's power is raised to 35.
