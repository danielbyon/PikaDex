Max Flutterby (Japanese: ダイワーム Dai Worm) is a damage-dealing Bug-type Max Move introduced in Generation VIII.

Contents

  • 2 Description
  • 3 Learnset
    • 3.1 Generation VIII

Effect

Max Fluttery inflicts damage and then decreases the target's and its ally's Special Attack by one stage. Its power and whether it is a special or physical move depends on the move it is based on.

Description

Games Description
SwSh This is a Bug-type attack Dynamax Pokémon use. This lowers the target's Sp. Atk stat.


Learnset

Generation VIII

Any Dynamax Pokémon can use Max Flutterby if it knows a damaging Bug-type move.

In the anime

Gigantamax Centiskorch
The Dynamaxed user attacks the opponent with a massive burst of Bug-type energy.

User First Used In Notes
Centiskorch Multicolored sparkles of light appear around Gigantamax Centiskorch's upper body and form into light green orbs of energy. The orbs then burst, releasing blasts of light green energy and multicolored butterfly-shaped masses of energy at the opponent.
