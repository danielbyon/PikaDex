Natural Gift (Japanese: しぜんのめぐみ Nature's Blessing) is a Normal-type move introduced in Generation IV. It was TM83 in Generation IV.

Effect

Generation IV

Natural Gift inflicts damage and has no secondary effect. Natural Gift's power and type vary depending on the user's held Berry. Using Natural Gift consumes the held Berry. The move will fail if the user is not holding a Berry, or if it cannot use its held Berry (due to Klutz, Magic Room, or Embargo).

If Natural Gift cannot be fully executed due to heavy rain or extremely harsh sunlight, the Berry is not consumed.

Natural Gift is affected by Normalize.

Generations V to VII

Natural Gift is unaffected by Normalize, Pixilate, Refrigerate, and Aerilate. However, it is affected by Ion Deluge (if the move would be Normal-type) and Electrify.

If a Red Card forces the user to leave the battlefield, the Berry is not consumed. If Powder prevents the move from being executed, the Berry is not consumed.

Consuming a berry via Natural Gift has nothing to do with Unnerve, Cheek Pouch, or Belch.

Generation VIII

Natural Gift cannot be selected in a battle.

In Explorers of Time, Darkness and Sky, Natural Gift is a move with 88% accuracy, and 17 PP. The user attacks enemy in the front. The move's type and power will change if it holds a Seed or a Berry, though unlike in main series, it can be used without an item.

Trivia

  • For the Cram-o-matic, every Berry uses the same type as the type the Natural Gift gets converted to with that Berry, and its value correlates to Natural Gift's power in Generation VII:
    • 2 Cram-o-matic points: 80 power, except damage-reducing Berries and Berries that may cause confusion
    • 4 Cram-o-matic points: 80 power, only damage-reducing Berries and Berries that may cause confusion
    • 6 Cram-o-matic points: 90 power
    • 10 Cram-o-matic points: 100 power
