Dragon Claw (Japanese: ドラゴンクロー Dragon Claw) is a damage-dealing Dragon-type move introduced in Generation III. It was TM02 from Generation III to Pokémon Ultra Sun and Ultra Moon, and is TR47 in Generation VIII.

Effect

Dragon Claw inflicts damage and has no secondary effect.

Dragon Claw can be used as part of a Pokémon Contest combination, with the user gaining two extra appeal points if any of the moves DragonBreath, Dragon Dance or Dragon Rage was used in the prior turn.

Pokémon Mystery Dungeon series

In Red Rescue Team and Blue Rescue Team, and Explorers of Time, Darkness and Sky, Dragon Claw is a move with 16 base power, 88% accuracy, and 10 PP. The user attacks enemy in the front.

In Gates to Infinity, Dragon Claw hits adjacent enemies when used by a Gigantic Pokémon.
