Dark Pulse (Japanese: あくのはどう Evil Pulse) is a damage-dealing Dark-type move introduced in Generation IV. It was TM79 in Generation IV, TM97 in Generations VI and VII, and TM20 in Pokémon: Let's Go, Pikachu! and Let's Go, Eevee!. It is TR58 in Generation VIII.

Effect

Generations IV-VII

Dark Pulse inflicts damage and has a 20% chance of causing the target to flinch and can hit non-adjacent opponents in Triple Battles.

Dark Pulse's power is increased by 50% when used by a Pokémon with the Ability Mega Launcher.

Generation VIII

In Pokémon Sword and Shield, Brilliant Diamond and Shining Pearl, Dark Pulse retains its effects from earlier generations.

In Pokémon Legends: Arceus, Dark Pulse's PP was lowered from 15 to 10, and the move can no longer cause flinching. Instead, it bypasses accuracy and evasion checks to always hit, unless the target is in the semi-invulnerable turn of a move such as Fly or Dig.

Description

Games Description
MDTDS Inflicts damage on enemies within a 1-tile range. It may also afflict enemies with a Cringe status condition, which makes its actions fail.

MDGtI It damages nearby enemies. It could also cause the Cringe status, which makes the enemy unable to attack or use moves.
SMD It damages nearby enemies. It could also make them flinch.
