Mystical Fire (Japanese: マジカルフレイム Magical Flame) is a damage-dealing Fire-type move introduced in Generation VI. In Pokémon X and Y, it was the signature move of Delphox. It is TM92 as of Generation VIII.

Effect

Generation VI

Mystical Fire inflicts damage and lowers the target's Special Attack stat by one stage.

Generation VII

Mystical Fire had its power increased from 65 to 75.
