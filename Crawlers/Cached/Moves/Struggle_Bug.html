Struggle Bug (Japanese: むしのていこう Insect Opposition) is a damage-dealing Bug-type move introduced in Generation V. It is TM76 in Generations V and VI.

Effect

Generation V

Struggle Bug inflicts damage and lowers the target's Special Attack stat by one stage. It also affects both opponents in Double Battles and all adjacent opponents in Triple Battles.

Generation VI

Struggle Bug's power changed from 30 to 50.
