Heart Stamp (Japanese: ハートスタンプ Heart Stamp) is a damage-dealing Psychic-type move introduced in Generation V.

Effect

Generations V to VII

Heart Stamp inflicts damage and has a 30% chance of causing the target to flinch.

Generation VIII

Heart Stamp cannot be selected in a battle.
