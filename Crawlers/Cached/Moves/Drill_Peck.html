Drill Peck (Japanese: ドリルくちばし Drill Beak) is a damage-dealing Flying-type move introduced in Generation I.

Effect

Drill Peck inflicts damage and can hit non-adjacent opponents in Triple Battles.

Drill Peck can also be used as part of a Pokémon Contest combination, with the user gaining extra four appeal points if move Peck was used in the prior turn.
