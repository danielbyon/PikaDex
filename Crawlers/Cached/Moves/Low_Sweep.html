Low Sweep (Japanese: ローキック Low Kick) is a damage-dealing Fighting-type move introduced in Generation V.

It is:

  • TM47 from Generation V to Pokémon Ultra Sun and Ultra Moon.
  • TM75 in Pokémon Sword and Shield.
  • TM27 in Pokémon Brilliant Diamond and Shining Pearl.
  • TM039 in Generation IX.

Effect

Generation V

Low Sweep inflicts damage and lowers the target's Speed stat by one stage.

Generation VI onwards

Low Sweep's power changed from 60 to 65.

In Pokémon UNITE, Low Sweep is Cinderace's second move. The user makes a swift low sweep in the designated direction, damaging any enemy it hits. After reaching level 8, this move is replaced by either Flame Charge or Feint.

Trivia

  • Low Sweep appears to be inspired by an attack called a leg sweep, which is a mainstay attack in a wide variety of martial arts. It is designed to trip opponents.
  • Low Sweep is the only Fighting-type Clever move.
