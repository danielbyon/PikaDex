Night Daze (Japanese: ナイトバースト Night Burst) is a damage-dealing Dark-type move introduced in Generation V. Prior to Generation VII, it was the signature move of the Zorua line. In Generation VIII, it is the signature move of Lunala.

Effect

Night Daze deals damage and has a 40% chance of lowering the target's accuracy by one stage.
