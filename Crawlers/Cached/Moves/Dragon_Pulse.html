Dragon Pulse (Japanese: りゅうのはどう Dragon Pulse) is a damage-dealing Dragon-type move introduced in Generation IV. It was TM59 in Generation IV and TM34 in Pokémon: Let's Go, Pikachu! and Let's Go, Eevee!.

Effect

Generations IV and V

Dragon Pulse inflicts damage and can hit non-adjacent opponents in Triple Battles.

Generation VI

Dragon Pulse's base power is reduced from 90 to 85. Additionally, its power is boosted by 50% if the user has the Ability Mega Launcher.

Other effects

A Poipole that knows this move will evolve into Naganadel when leveled up.
