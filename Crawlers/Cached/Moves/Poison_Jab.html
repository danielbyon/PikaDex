Poison Jab (Japanese: どくづき Poison Stab) is a damage-dealing Poison-type move introduced in Generation IV. It was TM84 in Generations IV through Pokémon Ultra Sun and Ultra Moon and TM26 in Pokémon: Let's Go, Pikachu! and Let's Go, Eevee!. It is TR57 in Generation VIII and TM84 in Pokémon Brilliant Diamond and Shining Pearl.
