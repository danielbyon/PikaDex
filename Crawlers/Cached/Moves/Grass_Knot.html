Grass Knot (Japanese: くさむすび Grass Knot) is a damage-dealing Grass-type move introduced in Generation IV that deals varying damage. It shares the same mechanic as the move Low Kick. It is TM86 from Generation IV to Generation VII and Pokémon Brilliant Diamond and Shining Pearl. It is TR77 in Pokémon Sword and Shield.
