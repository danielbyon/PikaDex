Leaf Storm (Japanese: リーフストーム Leaf Storm) is a damage-dealing Grass-type move introduced in Generation IV. It is TR71 in Generation VIII.

Effect

Generations IV and V

Leaf Storm deals damage, and lowers the user's Special Attack by two stages.

Generation VI onwards

Leaf Storm's power changed from 140 to 130.

Generation VIII

Pokémon Legends: Arceus

Leaf Storm's power has been lowered to 110. It lowers the user's offensive stats for three turns.

Pokémon Mystery Dungeon series

In Explorers of Time, Darkness and Sky, Leaf Storm is a move with 27 base power, 5 PP and 80% accuracy. The attack travels up to 10 tiles away and hits the first Pokémon it encounters, ally or enemy. The user's Special Attack is then reduced by two levels.

Pokémon UNITE

In Pokémon UNITE, Leaf Storm is Decidueye's first move. It is obtained by reaching level 8 and upgrading Astonish into it instead of Shadow Sneak. The user creates a tempest of sharp leaves, dealing damage to enemies it hits and decreases their movement speed by 30% for a short time. At point-blank range, it also shoves and stuns enemies hit. At level 13, the movement speed reduction's duration is increased.

Trivia

Sandy Cloak Wormadam using Leaf Storm in Black and White
  • Prior to Generation VI, Leaf Storm was the only Grass-type Cute move.
  • In Pokémon Black and White, School Kid Ann on Route 12 has a Sandy Cloak Wormadam that knows Leaf Storm, despite it being a move that Sandy Cloak Wormadam cannot learn by any means; it is instead learned by Plant Cloak Wormadam. Instead of Leaf Storm, the Sandy Cloak Wormadam should know Fissure.
