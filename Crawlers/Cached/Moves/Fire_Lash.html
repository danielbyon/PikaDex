Fire Lash (Japanese: ほのおのムチ Flame Whip) is a damage-dealing Fire-type move introduced in Generation VII. Prior to Generation VIII, it was the signature move of Heatmor. In Generation IX, it is the signature move of Salazzle.

Effect

Fire Lash inflicts damage and lowers the target's Defense stat by one stage.

Generation VIII

Pokémon Brilliant Diamond and Shining Pearl

Fire Lash cannot be selected in a battle.
