Breakneck Blitz (Japanese: ウルトラダッシュアタック Ultra Dash Attack) is a damage-dealing Normal-type Z-Move introduced in Generation VII.

Effect

Generation VII

Breakneck Blitz inflicts damage. Its power and whether it is a special or physical move depends on the move it is based on.

Breakneck Blitz is unaffected by Pixilate, Refrigerate, Aerilate, and Galvanize. However, it is affected by Ion Deluge and Electrify.

If Breakneck Blitz is used during weather with Weather Ball as its base move, it will turn into the Z-Move of the type corresponding to the type Weather Ball would be in that weather. This does not occur if Weather Ball is turned into a Z-Move via another move (e.g. via Z-Sleep Talk).

Learnset

Any non-Mega Evolved, non-Primal Pokémon can use Breakneck Blitz if it knows a damaging Normal-type move, holds a Normalium Z, and if its Trainer wears a Z-RingSM or Z-Power RingUSUM.
