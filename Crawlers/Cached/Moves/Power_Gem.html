Power Gem (Japanese: パワージェム Power Gem) is a damage-dealing Rock-type move introduced in Generation IV. It was TR63 in Pokémon Sword and Shield, and is TM101 in Generation IX.

Generations IV and V

Power Gem inflicts damage and has no secondary effect.

Power Gem's base power is 70.

Generation VI onwards

Power Gem's power changed from 70 to 80.
