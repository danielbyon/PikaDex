Astral Barrage (Japanese: アストラルビット Astral Bit) is a damage-dealing Ghost-type move introduced in Generation VIII. It is the signature move of Shadow Rider Calyrex.

Effect

Astral Barrage inflicts damage to all adjacent opponents.
