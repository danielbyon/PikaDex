Energy Ball (Japanese: エナジーボール Energy Ball) is a damage-dealing Grass-type move introduced in Generation IV. It was TM53 in Generations IV through VII. In Generation VIII, it is TR65.

Effect

Generations IV and V

Energy Ball damages the opponent and has a 10% chance of lowering the target's Special Defense by one stage.

Generation VI

Energy Ball's power was increased from 80 to 90. Additionally, it does not affect targets with Bulletproof.

Generation VIII

In Sword, Shield, Brilliant Diamond, and Shining Pearl, Energy Ball works identically to how it did in previous generations.

Pokémon Legends: Arceus

Energy Ball's base power has been lowered back to 80. It also has a 20% chance of lowering the target's defensive stats for three turns.

In Explorers of Time, Darkness and Sky, Energy Ball has 16 base power, 11 PP and 88% accuracy. The attack travels up to 10 tiles away and hits the first Pokémon it encounters, ally or enemy, possibly lowering their Special Defense by one stage.
