Effect

Peck inflicts damage and can hit non-adjacent opponents in Triple Battles.

Peck can be used as the first move of a Pokémon Contest combination, causing Drill Peck and Fury Attack to score double the normal appeal if used in the next turn.

In other games

Pokémon Mystery Dungeon series

In Red Rescue Team and Blue Rescue Team, Peck is a move with base power of 4, 22 PP and 95% accuracy. The user attacks enemy in the front.

In Explorers of Time, Explorers of Darkness and Explorers of Sky, the move's PP has been increased to 26.

In Pokémon UNITE, Peck is Talonflame's first move as Fletchling. The user flies toward a target Pokémon and attacks it three times. After evolving into Fletchinder at level 7, this move is replaced by either Flame Charge or Aerial Ace.
