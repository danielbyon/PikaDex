Work Up (Japanese: ふるいたてる Cheer Up) is a non-damaging Normal-type move introduced in Generation V. It was TM83 in Generation V before losing its status in Generation VI. It regained its TM status in Generation VII as TM01. In Pokémon Sword and Shield, it is TR85, while in Pokémon Brilliant Diamond and Shining Pearl, it is TM10.

Effect

Work Up raises the user's Attack and Special Attack stats by one stage each.

If powered up by a Normalium Z into Z-Work Up, the user's Attack stat is raised by an additional one stage.
