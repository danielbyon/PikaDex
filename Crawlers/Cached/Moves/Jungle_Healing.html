Jungle Healing (Japanese: ジャングルヒール Jungle Heal) is a non-damaging Grass-type move introduced in Generation VIII. It is the signature move of Zarude.

Effect

Jungle Healing restores the HP of the user and its allies by 25% of their maximum HP, and heals any non-volatile status conditions they may have.

Description

Games Description
SwShLA The user becomes one with the jungle, restoring HP and healing any status conditions of itself and its ally Pokémon in battle.
BDSP This move can’t be used. It’s recommended that this move is forgotten. Once forgotten, this move can’t be remembered.

In the anime

Zarude
The user becomes one with the jungle, healing the target.
Pokémon Method
User First Used In Notes
Zarude Zarude stands over the target and extends several green vines from its back, wrapping them around nearby trees. The vines on Zarude's back and around its arms then glow light green, and the ground around Zarude also glows light green, healing the target.
Dada Secrets of the Jungle Debut


  • In Secrets of the Jungle, Koko used Jungle Healing, marking the first time a human has used a Pokémon move in the anime.

Trivia

  • This move originated as a TCG attack.
