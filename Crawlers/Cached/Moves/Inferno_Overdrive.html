Inferno Overdrive (Japanese: ダイナミックフルフレイム Dynamic Full Flame) is a damage-dealing Fire-type Z-Move introduced in Generation VII.

Effect

Inferno Overdrive inflicts damage. Its power and whether it is a special or physical move depends on the move it is based on.

If Breakneck Blitz is used during harsh sunlight with Weather Ball as its base move, it will turn into Inferno Overdrive. This does not occur if Weather Ball is turned into a Z-Move via another move like Sleep Talk.

Description

Games Description
SMUSUM The user breathes a stream of intense fire toward the target with the full force of its Z-Power. The power varies depending on the original move.
SwSh This move can't be used. It's recommended that this move is forgotten. Once forgotten, this move can't be remembered.

Learnset

Any Pokémon can use Inferno Overdrive if it knows a damaging Fire-type move, holds a Firium Z, and if its Trainer wears a Z-RingSM or Z-Power RingUSUM.

Trivia

  • An Inferno Overdrive based on V-create has the highest power of all Fire-type moves and all Z-Moves.
  • Inferno Overdrive was the first Z-Move seen in the anime and the Pokémon Adventures manga.
