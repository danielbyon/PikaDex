Echoed Voice (Japanese: エコーボイス Echo Voice) is a damage-dealing Normal-type move introduced in Generation V. It was TM49 from Generation V to Pokémon Ultra Sun and Ultra Moon.

Effect

Generation V

Echoed Voice deals damage. Its base power increases by 40 each consecutive turn it is used but reaches a maximum power at 200. However, the power will not increase during a round and will only increase once per round (regardless of how many Pokémon used it). The base power of Echoed Voice does not reset upon reaching its maximum. When failing, the base power will still increase as long as the move is executed, such as when it misses or is prevented by Protect. Pokémon with the Ability Soundproof are not affected by this move.

Generation VI onwards

Substitute no longer protects against this move.

Echoed Voice will become a Water-type move when used by a Pokémon with the Ability Liquid Voice. If the user is affected by Throat Chop, it will be unable to use this move for two turns.

Echoed Voice will have its power boosted by 30% when used by a Pokémon with the Ability Punk Rock. The damage dealt by Echoed Voice will also be halved when used against a Pokémon with Punk Rock. If the user is holding a Throat Spray, the item will be consumed and the user's Special Attack stat will raise by one stage.

Pokémon Mystery Dungeon series

Echoed Voice damages all enemies adjacent to the user. Its power is increased by 50% if used by a teammate on the same floor during the same turn. In Pokémon Super Mystery Dungeon, its power is also increased if it was the last move used by the user on the same floor.

Trivia

  • Echoed Voice can reach the highest base power of all sound-based moves and the highest base power of all moves with no negative effects on the user, excluding Z-Moves. If used a sufficient amount of turns, its base power will reach 200.
    • This can further be increased by Punk Rock, where it will reach a base power of 260.
