Gunk Shot (Japanese: ダストシュート Dust Shoot) is a damage-dealing Poison-type move introduced in Generation IV. It is TR73 in Generation VIII.

Effect

Generations IV and V

Gunk Shot deals damage and has a 30% chance of poisoning the target.

Generation VI onwards

Gunk Shot's accuracy was changed from 70% to 80%.

In other games

Pokémon Mystery Dungeon series

In Explorers of Time, Darkness and Sky, Gunk Shot is a move with 22 base power, 6 PP and 80% accuracy. The attack travels up to 10 tiles away and hits the first Pokémon it encounters, ally or enemy, possibly inflicting poison.
