Lash Out (Japanese: うっぷんばらし Vent Anger) is a damage-dealing Dark-type move introduced in Generation VIII.

Contents

  • 1 Effect
  • 2 Description
  • 3 Learnset
    • 3.1 By Move Tutor
  • 4 In other languages

Effect

Lash Out deals damage. If any of the user's stats were lowered during the turn this move is used, its base power is doubled to 150.

Description

Games Description
SwSh The user lashes out to vent its frustration toward the target. If its stats were lowered during the turn, the power of this move is doubled.
BDSP This move can’t be used. It’s recommended that this move is forgotten. Once forgotten, this move can’t be remembered.
