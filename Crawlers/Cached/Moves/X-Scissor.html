X-Scissor (Japanese: シザークロス Scissor Cross) is a damage-dealing Bug-type move introduced in Generation IV. It has been TM81 since Generation IV, except in Pokémon: Let's Go, Pikachu! and Let's Go, Eevee!, where it is TM24.
