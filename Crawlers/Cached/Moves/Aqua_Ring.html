Aqua Ring (Japanese: アクアリング Aqua Ring) is a non-damaging Water-type move introduced in Generation IV.

Effect

Generation IV

The user surrounds itself with a veil of water, allowing it to restore 1/16 of its max HP (rounded down) at the end of every turn. The effect of Aqua Ring can be Baton Passed, and will heal any Pokémon, regardless of its type.

If a Pokémon is holding a Big Root when Aqua Ring restores its HP, the amount of HP restored is increased by 30%.

Generation V onward

Aqua Ring can now be affected by Snatch.

If powered up by a Waterium Z into Z-Aqua Ring, the user's Defense stat rises by one stage.

Pokémon Mystery Dungeon series

In Explorers of Time, Darkness and Sky, Aqua Ring is a move with 20PP. It causes the user to gain the Aqua Ring status for 9-16 turns, causing user to gain 8HP every 4 turns. This move is affected is affected by Snatch and Taunt.

Pokémon Rumble Blast

The Pokémon pauses for about two seconds, then it restores HP by 2s and 3s.
