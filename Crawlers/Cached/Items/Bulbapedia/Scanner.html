The Scanner (Japanese: たんちき Detector) is a Key Item introduced in Generation III.

Effect

Pokémon Ruby, Sapphire, and Emerald

The Scanner can be given to Captain Stern in exchange for one of the items associated with Clamperl and its evolutions; DeepSeaScale or DeepSeaTooth.

Pokémon Omega Ruby and Alpha Sapphire

The Scanner can be given to Captain Stern in exchange for a Clear BellOR or Tidal BellAS, which can be used to encounter Ho-OhOR or LugiaAS somewhere on Sea Mauville.

In the manga

The Scanner in Pokémon Adventures

In the Pokémon Adventures manga

The Scanner first appeared in Adding It Up with Plusle & Minun II, where Courtney and Tabitha managed to steal it from the Abandoned Ship, despite the intervention of Ruby and Sapphire. Blaise later modified the Scanner, fixing the broken equipment, and enabling it to track the Red and Blue Orbs on Mt. Pyre. It reappeared under his ownership during the Omega Ruby & Alpha Sapphire chapter.
