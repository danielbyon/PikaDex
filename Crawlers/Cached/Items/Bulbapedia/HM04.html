HM04

From Bulbapedia, the community-driven Pokémon encyclopedia.
Jump to navigationJump to search
If you were looking for the glitch move, see HM04 (move).

HMs are TMs that serve a purpose out of battle, as well as being moves for in-battle use; however, with the exception of Generation V, in order to use an HM's purpose out of battle, a Gym Badge is required. Through Generation VI, HM04 is Strength. From Generation VII onward, HMs are not used and HM04 does not exist.

This HM was not available before Generation IV
