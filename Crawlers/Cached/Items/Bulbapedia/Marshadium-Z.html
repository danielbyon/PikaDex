Introduced in Generation VII
Pocket
Generation VII Z-Crystals

The Marshadium Z (Japanese: マーシャドーＺ Marshadow Z) is a type of Z-Crystal introduced in Pokémon Sun and Moon. It is a Pokémon-specific Z-Crystal associated with Marshadow and allows it to upgrade its Spectral Thief into the special Z-Move Soul-Stealing 7-Star Strike.

In the core series games

Price

Games Cost Sell price
SMUSUM N/A N/A

Effect

Bag item

Can create Marshadium Z held items for the player's Pokémon.

Held item

In battle

Allows Marshadow (or a Pokémon transformed into Marshadow) to upgrade its Spectral Thief into the special Z-Move Soul-Stealing 7-Star Strike.

Unaffected by item negating effects (such as Embargo and Klutz). Item manipulating effects (such as Trick and Fling) cannot remove a Z-Crystal from a Pokémon.

Rayquaza cannot Mega Evolve if it holds a Z-Crystal.

Outside of battle

A held Marshadium Z is automatically removed if the Pokémon is traded between players.

Description

Bag item

Games Description
SMUSUM It converts Z-Power into crystals that upgrade Marshadow's Spectral Thief to an exclusive Z-Move.

Held item

Games Description
SMUSUM This is a crystallized form of Z-Power. It upgrades Marshadow's Spectral Thief to a Z-Move.

Acquisition

Games Method
SM Bundled with Mount Tensei Marshadow (event)
