The Health Feather (Japanese: たいりょくのハネ Strength Feather), known as Health Wing prior to Generation VIII, is a type of medicine introduced in Generation V. It is a feather that slightly increases the HP of a Pokémon.

Effect

When used from the Bag on a Pokémon, adds 1 HP EV to the target Pokémon per use. It also increases the Pokémon's friendship a little.

The Health Feather is consumed upon use and cannot be used in battle.

In the anime

Feathers in the anime

In Reunion Battles In Nimbasa!, a Health Feather was included in the full set of feathers promised as a prize for winning the Club Battle tournament. In Club Battle Finale: A Hero's Outcome!, the set of feathers was won by Iris, who later sent them to the Village of Dragons.
