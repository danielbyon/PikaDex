The Paralyze Heal (Japanese: まひなおし Paralysis Heal), called Parlyz Heal prior to Generation VI, is a type of medicine introduced in Generation I. It can be used to cure a Pokémon from paralysis.

Wonder Launcher

The Parlyz Heal appears as a Wonder Launcher item. It costs 4 points to use.

In spin-off games

Pokémon Conquest

In Pokémon Conquest, the Parlyz Heal can be used to cure a Pokémon from paralysis.

A Parlyz Heal can be purchased from any Shop for 200-140 gold, and sold for 100-130 gold.

Description

Games Description
Conq. Cures paralysis.

In the anime

Though it did not physically appear in the anime, Paralyze Heal was mentioned in A Pokémon Scent-sation. Erika offered to give some of it to Ash's Bulbasaur after it had been hit by her Tangela's Stun Spore, but Ash declined the offer.

In the manga

A Paralyze Heal in Pokémon Adventures

In the Pokémon Adventures manga

In Weezing Winks Out, Koga healed Blue, who had been paralyzed by Agatha's Gengar's Lick, with a Paralyze Heal sent to him inside one of his shuriken Poké Balls.

Trivia

  • In Pokémon Adventures, Blue is healed with a Paralyze Heal, the first time a status condition healing item besides local specialties is used on a human.
