Excite Scent

Exciting Fragrance
Pokémon Global Link artwork
Introduced in Generation III
Pocket
Generation III Items (Cologne Case)

Excite Scent (Japanese: ワクワクのかおり Exciting Fragrance) is an item exclusive to the Orre-based games, Pokémon Colosseum and XD: Gale of Darkness. It is one of the Scents that can be used to help in purifying Shadow Pokémon.

Joy Scent and Vivid Scent are alternate versions of this item.
