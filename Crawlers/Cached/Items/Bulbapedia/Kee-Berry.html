A Kee Berry (Japanese: アッキのみ Acki Fruit) is a type of Berry introduced in Generation VI.

Growth and harvest

Generation VI

A Kee Berry will mature from a planted seed to a full-grown, fruit-bearing tree in 96 hours, with 16 hours per stage. A Kee tree will yield 1-10 Berries. During its growth, watering the plant will add 0.2 Berries to the final harvest, weeding it will add 0.1, and removing a pest will add 1.

Generation VII

A Kee Berry will mature from a planted seed to a full-grown, fruit-bearing tree in 72 hours. A Kee tree will yield 2-8 Berries.

Uses

In-battle

A Kee Berry, when held by a Pokémon, will increase their Defense by one stage the first time they are struck by a physical move. It is consumed once used.

Juice Shoppe

If blended with a Maranga Berry at the Lumiose Juice Shoppe, it will become Perilous Soup, resetting the drinker's EVs to 0.

Mulch

The Kee Berry has an additional effect in the growing of Berries. When used to make Mulch, it produces Amaze Mulch, which combines the effects of Boost Mulch, Rich Mulch, and Surprise Mulch, greatly improving the yield of Berries and the chance for mutations.
