The Voice Cases (Japanese: こえカプセル Voice Capsule) are Key Items exclusive to Pokémon XD: Gale of Darkness. They are a series of inventions made by Dr. Kaminko.

In the games

Effect

Each of the Voice Cases contains the cry of a Pokémon with a malfunctioning Pokémon Translator.

  • Voice Case 1: Combusken in Agate Village
  • Voice Case 2: Poochyena in Agate Village
  • Voice Case 3: Taillow in Agate Village
  • Voice Case 4: Wobbuffet in Agate Village
  • Voice Case 5: Shroomish at ONBS

When the Pokémon hears its own cry played by the Voice Case, the Translator will come off. The only exception to this is Wobbuffet, who is unaffected by the sound of its Voice Case.
