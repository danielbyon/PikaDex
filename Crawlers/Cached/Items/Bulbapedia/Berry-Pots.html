The Berry Pots (Japanese: きのみプランター Berry Planter) are Key Items introduced in Generation IV.

Effect

The Berry Pots are portable planters that allow the player to plant, water, and grow Berry trees while traveling.

In the anime

Berry Pots in the anime

In An Egg Scramble!, Berry Pots were one of the things Khoury was selling at the Johto Festival.
