The Wailmer Pail (Japanese: ホエルコじょうろ Hoeruko Watering Can) is a Key Item introduced in Generation III. It is a watering can fashioned after a Wailmer.

Effect

The Wailmer Pail allows the player to water planted Berries. If watering is done correctly, the resulting Berry trees will have higher amounts of Berries to pick and store.

In Pokémon Emerald only, the Wailmer Pail also allows the player to water a fake tree in the Battle Frontier, revealing it to be a wild Sudowoodo that attacks the player.

Trivia

  • The Wailmer Pail is the only watering can whose design is based on a Pokémon that was also introduced in the same generation as the watering can.
