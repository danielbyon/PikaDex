A Ganlon Berry (Japanese: リュガのみ Ryuga Fruit) is a type of Berry introduced in Generation III.

Growth and harvest

Generation III

A Ganlon Berry will mature from a planted seed to a full-grown, fruit-bearing tree in 96 hours, with 24 hours per stage. A Ganlon tree will yield 1-2 Berries.

Generation IV

A Ganlon Berry will mature from a planted seed to a full-grown, fruit-bearing tree in 96 hours, with 24 hours per stage. A Ganlon tree will yield 1-5 Berries.

Generation VI

A Ganlon Berry will mature from a planted seed to a full-grown, fruit-bearing tree in 96 hours, with 16 hours per stage. A Ganlon tree will yield 1-10 Berries. During its growth, watering the plant will add 0.2 Berries to the final harvest, weeding it will add 0.1, and removing a pest will add 1.

Generation VII

A Ganlon Berry will mature from a planted seed to a full-grown, fruit-bearing tree in 72 hours. A Ganlon tree will yield 2-8 Berries.

Berry Crushing

At 100% performance, a Ganlon Berry will contribute 1000 units of powder.

Poffin cooking

At 100% performance, a Ganlon Berry can produce a Level 40 (maybe higher) Bitter-Dry Poffin when cooking alone.
