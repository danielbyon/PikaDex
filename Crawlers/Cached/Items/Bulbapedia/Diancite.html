The Diancite (Japanese: ディアンシナイト Diancinite) is a type of held item introduced in Pokémon Omega Ruby and Alpha Sapphire. It is the Mega Stone that allows Diancie to Mega Evolve into Mega Diancie.

Effect

In battle

If held by a Diancie, allows it to Mega Evolve into Mega Diancie if its Trainer has a Key Stone. Each Trainer can only Mega Evolve one Pokémon per battle.

Unaffected by item negating effects (such as Embargo and Klutz). Item manipulating effects (such as Trick and Fling) cannot give Diancie a Diancite or remove a Diancite from Diancie.

Outside of battle

If held by a Diancie, allows it to Mega Evolve into Mega Diancie when it performs a Spectacular Talent during a Pokémon Contest Spectacular while its Trainer has a Key Stone. Diancie will remain a Mega Diancie until the Talent Round ends.

Pokémon holding Diancite in Pokémon Omega Ruby and Alpha Sapphire cannot be traded to Pokémon X and Y.

In spin-off games

Pokémon Shuffle

In Pokémon Shuffle, the Diancite allows the player to Mega Evolve Diancie and Shiny Diancie.

The player can obtain the Diancite by passing Level 50 on a Mega Diancie Escalation Battle in Weeks 10 and 11 of the event rotation.

The Diancite was also available as a prize during the Mega Diancie Escalation Battles prior to the event rotation system. This Escalation Battle ran on both the Nintendo 3DS and Mobile versions from February 9 to 23, 2016; August 23 to September 6, 2016; April 11 to 25, 2017; August 22 to September 5, 2017; and December 12 to 26, 2017.

In the manga

A Diancite in Pokémon Adventures

In the Pokémon Adventures manga

In Omega Alpha Adventure 21, Ruby used Hoopa's ring to find a Diancite for Diancie to use in a Pokémon Contest Spectacular. This allowed him to Mega Evolve Diancie for his and Sapphire's performance.
