Crafting

From Bulbapedia, the community-driven Pokémon encyclopedia.
(Redirected from Recipe)
Jump to navigationJump to search
Crafting Kit in Pokémon Legends: Arceus

Crafting (Japanese: クラフト Craft) is a game mechanic in Pokémon Legends: Arceus. It allows creating items from raw materials, and using basic items to create more advanced items.

Crafting requires either a crafting bench or a Crafting Kit. When using the Crafting Kit, only items in the player's satchel can be used, and the player must have room in their satchel for the new item in order to craft it; conversely, when using a crafting bench, the player can use items in either their satchel or storage as materials, and the crafted item is sent to storage if there is not room in their satchel.

Crafting benches can be found at the Craftworks in Jubilife Village and at Galaxy Team campsites.
