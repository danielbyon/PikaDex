The Sassy Mint (Japanese: なまいきミント Sassy Mint) is an item introduced in Generation VIII. It is one of the types of mints that change the effect of a Pokémon's Nature.

In the core series games

Price

Games Cost Sell price
SwShBDSP N/A 10

Effect

If used from the Bag, it changes the effect of a Pokémon's Nature on its stats to that of the Sassy Nature, increasing its Special Defense stat and decreasing its Speed stat.

Description

Games Description
SwShBDSP When a Pokémon smells this mint, its Sp. Def will grow more easily, but its Speed will grow more slowly.
