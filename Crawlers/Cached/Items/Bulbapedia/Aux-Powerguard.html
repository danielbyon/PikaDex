The Aux Powerguard (Japanese: マルチアップ Multi-Up) is a type of battle item introduced in Pokémon Legends: Arceus. It temporarily boosts the offensive and defensive stats of the Pokémon currently in battle.

In the core series games

Price

Games Cost Sell price
LA N/A 600

Effect

When used on a Pokémon from the Bag during battle, it sharply raises that Pokémon's the offensive and defensive stats. These effects disappear if the Pokémon leaves the battle.

Description

Games Description
LA An item that sharply boosts the offensive and defensive stats of a Pokémon during a battle. These effects disappear if the Pokémon leaves the battle.

Games Finite methods Repeatable methods
