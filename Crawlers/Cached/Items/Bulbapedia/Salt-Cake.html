The Salt Cake (Japanese: ミネラルよせだま Mineral Lure-Ball) is a type of item introduced in Pokémon Legends: Arceus.

Effect

When used from the Bag, it is potently effective at attracting Pokémon that bear a resemblance to grass, plants, or minerals.

Salt Cake require 1 Crunchy Salt and 1 Cake-Lure Base to craft.
