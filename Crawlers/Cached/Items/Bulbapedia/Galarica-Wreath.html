Galarica Wreath (Japanese: ガラナツリース Galanut Wreath) is a type of evolution item introduced in Generation VIII.

In the core series games

Price

Games Cost Sell price
SwSh N/A 3,000

Effect

Causes Galarian Slowpoke to evolve into Galarian Slowking upon use. This consumes the Galarica Wreath.

Description

Games Description
SwSh A wreath made from woven-together Galarica Twigs. Placing it on the head of a Galarian Slowpoke makes the Pokémon very happy.

Acquisition

Games Finite methods Repeatable methods
SwShCT Roaring-Sea Caves (from a woman within the cavern in exchange for fifteen Galarica Twigs)
