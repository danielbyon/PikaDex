(Japanese: ギネマのみ Ginema Fruit) is a Berry introduced in Generation III. It could only be acquired by scanning in the correct Japan-exclusive e-Reader card in Pokémon Ruby and Sapphire, and if transferred forward to Generation IV, becomes an Enigma Berry.

In Pokémon Ruby and Sapphire, the Trainer will not be permitted to enter the Pokémon Cable Club Trade Center if a Pokémon in their party is holding this Berry. A Pokémon holding this Berry cannot be traded to Pokémon Colosseum or Pokémon XD: Gale of Darkness, nor can it be stored in Pokémon Box Ruby & Sapphire.

Growth and Harvest

Generation III

A Ginema Berry will mature from a planted seed to a full-grown, fruit-bearing tree in 72 hours, with 18 hours per stage. A Ginema tree will yield 2-3 Berries.
