The Liberty Pass (Japanese: リバティチケット Liberty Ticket) is an event-exclusive Key Item that allows a player to travel to Liberty Garden where they will meet Victini in Pokémon Black and White.

In the core series games

Effect

Passive effect

Once the Liberty Pass is obtained, the player must take it to Liberty Pier, the westernmost pier of the Castelia City harbor. The captain of the boat docked here will recognize the Liberty Pass, and volunteer to take the player to Liberty Garden. If the player does not have the Liberty Pass, the vessel remains stationary.

Acquisition

Distribution

The Liberty Pass was distributed wirelessly at the release of Black and White in all geographic regions and in some retail outlets outside of Japan. The release length differs between countries.

In the manga

Liberty Passes in Pokémon Adventures

Pokémon Adventures

Black & White chapter

A pair of Liberty Passes appeared in At Liberty on Liberty Garden under the ownership of White. She and Black used them to board a ship from Castelia City to Liberty Garden in order to search for Victini per Professor Juniper's request.
