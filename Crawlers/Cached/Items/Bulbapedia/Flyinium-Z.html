The Flyinium Z (Japanese: ヒコウＺ Flying Z) is a type of Z-Crystal introduced in Pokémon Sun and Moon. It is the type-specific Z-Crystal associated with the Flying type and allows Pokémon to upgrade their Flying-type moves to Z-Moves.

In the core series games

Price

Effect

Bag item

Can create Flyinium Z held items for the player's Pokémon.

Held item

In battle

Allows the holder to upgrade its Flying-type damaging moves to the Z-Move Supersonic Skystrike or upgrade its Flying-type status moves into Z-Moves.

Unaffected by item negating effects (such as Embargo and Klutz). Item manipulating effects (such as Trick and Fling) cannot remove a Z-Crystal from a Pokémon.

Multitype changes an Arceus holding this item to its Flying-type form. Rayquaza cannot Mega Evolve if it holds a Z-Crystal.

Outside of battle

A held Flyinium Z is automatically removed if the Pokémon is traded between players.

Multitype changes an Arceus holding this item to its Flying-type form.

In the anime

A Flyinium Z in the anime

A Flyinium Z appeared in Run, Heroes, Run!, where Kiawe received it from Tapu Fini as a reward for clearing the trial the guardian deity had set up for him. Kiawe then proceeded to perform Supersonic Skystrike with his Charizard.

A full set of type-specific Z-Crystals, including the Flyinium Z, appeared in A Grand Debut!, where Hapu allowed Ash to choose his grand trial prize from them.

A Flyinium Z appeared in Beauty is Only Crystal Deep!, where it was one of the Z-Crystals stolen by a Pheromosa.

A full set of replicas of the type-specific Z-Crystals, including the Flyinium Z, appeared in A Team-on-Team Tussle!, where they were shown to Ash and his classmates by Professor Kukui.
