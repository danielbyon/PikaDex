The Bean Cake (Japanese: マメよせだま Bean Lure-Ball) is a type of item introduced in Pokémon Legends: Arceus.

Description

Games Description
LA A cake made by combining beans with a Cake-Lure Base. These cakes are potently effective at attracting fish Pokémon, as well as bird Pokémon and their flying ilk.

Acquisition

Games Finite methods Repeatable methods
LA Complete Request 53: "Octillery's Ink" Crafting: 1 Plump Beans and 1 Cake-Lure Base
Jubilife Village General Store, base camp stores
