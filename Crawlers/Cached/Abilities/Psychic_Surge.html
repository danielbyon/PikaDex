Psychic Surge (Japanese: サイコメイカー Psycho Maker) is an Ability introduced in Generation VII. Prior to Generation VIII, it was the signature Ability of Tapu Lele.

  • 2 Pokémon with Psychic Surge
  • 3 In other languages

Effect

In battle

When a Pokémon with Psychic Surge enters the battle, it creates the effect of the move Psychic Terrain on the battlefield.

Outside of battle

Psychic Surge has no effect outside of battle.
