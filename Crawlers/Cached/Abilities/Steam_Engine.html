Steam Engine (Ability)

From Bulbapedia, the community-driven Pokémon encyclopedia.
Jump to navigationJump to search
Steam Engine じょうききかん
Steam Engine
Flavor text
Generation VIII
Boosts the Pokémon's Speed stat drastically if hit by a Fire- or Water-type move.
Generation IX
Boosts the Speed stat drastically when the Pokémon is hit by a Fire- or Water-type move.

Steam Engine (Japanese: じょうききかん Steam Engine) is an Ability introduced in Generation VIII. It is the signature Ability of Rolycoly's evolutionary line.

Contents

  • 1 Effect
    • 1.1 In battle
    • 1.2 Outside of battle
  • 2 Pokémon with Steam Engine
  • 3 In other languages

Effect

In battle

When a Pokémon with Steam Engine is hit by a Fire- or Water-type move, its Speed stat is increased by six stages. This Ability activates after any stat decreases caused by the move are applied.

Outside of battle

If a Pokémon with Steam Engine is in the party (even if fainted), the number of cycles it takes for all Pokémon Eggs in the party to hatch are halved. This effect does not stack with itself, Flame Body, or Magma Armor.
