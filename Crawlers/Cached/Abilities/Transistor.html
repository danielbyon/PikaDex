Transistor (Japanese: トランジスタ Transistor) is an Ability introduced in Generation VIII. It is the signature Ability of Regieleki.

Effect

In battle

Transistor boosts the power of user's Electric-type moves by 50%.

Outside of battle

Transistor has no effect outside of battle.
