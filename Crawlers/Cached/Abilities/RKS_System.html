RKS System (Japanese: ＡＲシステム AR System) is an Ability introduced in Generation VII. It is the signature Ability of Silvally.

Effect

In battle

RKS System changes the type of the Pokémon with this Ability depending on the memory attached.

Role Play, Gastro Acid, Worry Seed, Simple Beam, and Entrainment fail if the target has the Ability RKS System. Skill Swap fails if either Pokémon has RKS System. RKS System cannot be copied by Trace, Power of Alchemy, or Receiver. RKS System cannot be replaced by Mummy or suppressed by Core Enforcer. If a Pokémon other than Silvally obtains RKS System with Imposter or Transform, the Pokémon will copy the form of the target Silvally regardless of its own held item.

Outside of battle

RKS System has no effect outside of battle.

In other games

Pokemon Masters EX

RKS System has been reworked into a skill-independent game mechanic that only Gladion and Silvally use. They can use their unique Trainer move once per battle to change Silvally's type, the type of their Multi-Attack, and the type of their and sync move into that of their targeted opponent's weakness. This also changes Gladion and Silvally's weakness type. After the battle, they return to their Normal typing.

Trivia

Icon for RKS Laboratories
  • The name of this Ability is a reference to Arceus, with its English name being similar to the pronunciation of "Arceus". In German, Spanish, French, and Italian, its name is instead a reference to Arceus's category, the Alpha Pokémon.
    • The company RKS Laboratories (Japanese: アルセウス製薬 Arceus Pharma) in Galar has a logo similar to the rings on Arceus's body, furthering the connection.
