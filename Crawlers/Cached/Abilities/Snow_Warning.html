Snow Warning (Japanese: ゆきふらし Snowfall) is an Ability introduced in Generation IV. Prior to Generation VI, it was the signature Ability of Snover and Abomasnow.

Effect

In battle

Generations IV and V

Snow Warning summons a hailstorm in battle as soon as a Pokémon with Snow Warning enters the battle. Its effects are the same as Hail, but instead of lasting for five turns, it lasts for the whole battle until replaced. If a Pokémon with Snow Warning is sent out in the same turn as another Pokémon whose Ability affects the weather, the slower Pokémon's Ability will override the faster Ability.

Generation VI

In Generation VI, the hail will last only for five turns instead. This can be extended to eight turns if the Pokémon is holding an Icy Rock.

If heavy rain, extremely harsh sunlight, or strong winds are present, Snow Warning will fail to activate.

Outside of battle

Snow Warning has no effect outside of battle.

In other games

Pokémon Mystery Dungeon series

In Explorers of Time, Darkness, Snow Warning changes the weather to Hail upon entering a floor, and then every subsequent 36 turns.

In Super Mystery Dungeon, Snow Warning changes the weather to hail upon entering a floor. Hail lasts a certain number of turns (in multiples of 66) before clearing. After the weather has cleared, Snow Warning will again activate after waiting a certain number of turns (in multiples of 66).

In the manga

Pokémon Adventures

  • Candice's Abomasnow and Snover are revealed to have Snow Warning as their Ability.

Trivia

  • Just like the move Hail debuted one generation after all three other weather moves (Sunny Day, Rain Dance, and Sandstorm), Snow Warning debuted one generation after the three other weather-summoning Abilities (Drought, Drizzle, and Sand Stream).
    • This makes Snow Warning the only weather-summoning Ability that was not introduced in Generation III.
