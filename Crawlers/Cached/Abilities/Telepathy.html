Telepathy (Japanese: テレパシー Telepathy) is an Ability introduced in Generation V.

Effect

In battle

A Pokémon with Telepathy avoids damaging moves used by its allies. This includes attacks with multiple targets (such as Discharge), as well as attacks exclusively targeting an ally with Telepathy (such as Thunderbolt). However, it is not protected from its allies' status moves (such as Teeter Dance or Confuse Ray).

When Telepathy prevents an attack from hitting, the message "<name> avoids attacks by its ally Pokémon!" will be displayed.

Outside of battle

Telepathy has no effect outside of battle.

In the manga

Pokémon Adventures

  • Colress's Beheeyem was revealed to have Telepathy as his Ability.

Trivia

  • Telepathy is the most common ability to have as a hidden ability.
  • In Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Red Rescue Team and Blue Rescue Team, Xatu uses Telepathy to communicate with the residents of the Pokémon Square, both before the Ability was introduced and with Xatu not being able to have Telepathy.
