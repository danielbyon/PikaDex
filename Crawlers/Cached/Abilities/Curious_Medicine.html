Curious Medicine (Japanese: きみょうなくすり Strange Medicine) is an Ability introduced in Generation VIII. It is the signature Ability of Galarian Slowking.

Effect

In battle

When a Pokémon with this Ability enters the battle, stat stages of ally Pokémon are reset to 0.
