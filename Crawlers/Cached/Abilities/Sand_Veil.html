Sand Veil (Japanese: すながくれ Hidden in Sand) is an Ability introduced in Generation III.

Effect

In battle

During a sandstorm, the accuracy of any move used against a Pokémon with this Ability is modified by a factor of 4/5. A Pokémon with this Ability will take no damage from the sandstorm if it otherwise would.

Outside of battle

From Pokémon Emerald onwards, if a Pokémon with Sand Veil is in the first place in the party (even if fainted) in an area with a sandstorm (such as Route 228 in Sinnoh), the chance of encountering a wild Pokémon is decreased by 50%.

In other games

Pokémon Mystery Dungeon series

In Red Rescue Team and Blue Rescue Team, Explorers of Time, Darkness, and Sky, Sand Veil increases evasion by two stages when active.

In the manga

Pokémon Adventures

  • In volume 40, Pearl's Digler was revealed to have Sand Veil as his Ability.
  • Red's Diglett and Sandshrew are revealed to have Sand Veil as their Ability.
  • Giovanni's Dugtrio is revealed to have Sand Veil as his Ability.
  • Wally's Cacturne is revealed to have Sand Veil as his Ability.
  • Cynthia's Garchomp is revealed to have Sand Veil as her Ability.

Trivia

  • Prior to becoming an Ability, Sand Veil appeared as a TCG Attack.
