Chlorophyll (Japanese: ようりょくそ Chlorophyll) is an Ability introduced in Generation III.

Effect

In battle

During harsh sunlight, the Speed stat of Pokémon with this Ability is doubled.

Outside of battle

Chlorophyll has no effect outside of battle.

In the manga

In the Pokémon Adventures manga

  • In volume 42, Gold's Sunbo was revealed to have Chlorophyll as her Ability.
  • Blue's Exeggutor is revealed to have Chlorophyll as his Ability.
  • Sapphire's Troppy is revealed to have Chlorophyll as his Ability.
  • Erika's Vileplume and Bellossom are revealed to have Chlorophyll as their Ability.
  • Gardenia's Cherubi is revealed to have Chlorophyll as her Ability.
  • Burgh's Leavanny is revealed to have Chlorophyll as its Ability.

Trivia

  • Every Pokémon with this Ability is weak to Fire-type attacks, which have increased power under harsh sunlight.
  • Every Pokémon to use this Ability in the anime has belonged to a Grass-type Gym Leader.
