Chlorophyll (Japanese: ようりょくそ Chlorophyll) is an Ability introduced in Generation III.

Effect

In battle

During harsh sunlight, the Speed stat of Pokémon with this Ability is doubled.

Outside of battle

Chlorophyll has no effect outside of battle.

In other games

Pokémon Mystery Dungeon series

In Sunny weather, Pokémon can use an attack twice in one turn, using only one PP. Any linked moves will also attack twice. Solar Beam, being a single-turn move in harsh sunlight, will also be executed twice.

In the manga

Pokémon Adventures

  • In volume 42, Gold's Sunflora, Sunbo, was revealed to have Chlorophyll as her Ability.
  • Red's Victreebel and Weepinbell were revealed to have Chlorophyll as their Ability.
  • Blue's Exeggutor is revealed to have Chlorophyll as his Ability.
  • Sapphire's Tropius, Troppy, is revealed to have Chlorophyll as his Ability.
  • Erika's Vileplume and Bellossom are revealed to have Chlorophyll as their Ability.
  • Gardenia's Cherubi is revealed to have Chlorophyll as her Ability.
  • Burgh's Leavanny is revealed to have Chlorophyll as its Ability.
  • A Team Plasma Grunt's Maractus was revealed to Chlorophyll as its Ability.
  • The Bellsprout belonging to the Sages are revealed to have Chlorophyll as their Ability.

Trivia

  • Every Pokémon with this Ability is weak to Fire-type attacks, which have increased power under harsh sunlight.
  • Every Pokémon to use this Ability in the anime has belonged to a Grass-type Gym Leader.
