Soul-Heart (Japanese: ソウルハート Soul-Heart) is an Ability introduced in Generation VII. It is the signature Ability of Magearna.

Contents

    • 1.1 In battle

  • 2 Pokémon with Soul-Heart
  • 3 In other languages

Effect

In battle

Soul-Heart raises the user's Special Attack by one stage every time another Pokémon faints.

Outside of battle

Soul-Heart has no effect outside of battle.
