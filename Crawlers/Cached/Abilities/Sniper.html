Sniper (Japanese: スナイパー Sniper) is an Ability introduced in Generation IV.

Effect

In battle

Generation IV

Critical hits will do 3× damage instead of 2× damage. Sniper cannot be activated by Beat Up.

Generation V

Sniper can now be activated by Beat Up.

Generation VI onward

Critical hits will now do 2.25× damage instead of 1.5× damage.

Outside of battle

Sniper has no effect outside of battle.

In other games

Pokémon Mystery Dungeon series

In Explorers of Time, Darkness and Sky, Sniper makes critical hits do 2× damage instead of 1.5× damage.

MDTDS Boosts the damage the Pokémon inflicts with critical hits.

Conq. Powers up moves if they become critical hits.

MDRTDX This Ability increases the damage of moves that result in critical hits.


In the manga

Pokémon Adventures

  • Yellow's Seadra is revealed to have Sniper as his Ability.

Trivia

  • All Pokémon that can have Sniper can learn at least Focus Energy or at least one move with a high critical-hit ratio.
