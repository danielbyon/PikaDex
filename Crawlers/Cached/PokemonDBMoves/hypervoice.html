Hyper Voice (move)

  • Contents

Move data

Type Normal
Category   Special
Power 90
Accuracy 100
PP 10  (max. 16)
Makes contact? No
Introduced Generation 3

Machine/Record

Sword​/​Shield TR42

Effects

Hyper Voice deals damage with no additional effect, besides targeting multiple foes.
Hyper Voice has no effect on Pokémon with the ability Soundproof.

Changes

  • In Generation 3, Hyper Voice is categorized as Physical.

Z-Move effects

When a Pokémon is holding Normalium Z and uses its Z-Power, Hyper Voice turns into Breakneck Blitz and has base power 175.
