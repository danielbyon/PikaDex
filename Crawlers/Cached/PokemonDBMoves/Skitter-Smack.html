Skitter Smack (move)

  • Contents
  • Flavor text
  • Learnt by move tutor

Move data

Type Bug
Category   Physical
Power 70
Accuracy 90
PP 10  (max. 16)
Makes contact? Yes
Introduced Generation 8

Effects

The user skitters behind the target to attack. This also lowers the target’s Sp. Atk stat.

Skitter Smack is a tutor move available in The Isle of Armor DLC.
