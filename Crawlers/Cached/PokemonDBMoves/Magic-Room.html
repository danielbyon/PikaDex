Move data

Type Psychic
Category   Status
Power —
Accuracy —
PP 10  (max. 16)
Makes contact? No
Introduced Generation 5

Machine/Record

Sword​/​Shield TM72

Effects

Magic Room suppresses the effects of held items for all Pokémon for five turns.

Changes

  • In Generation 5, Magic Room has a priority of -7.

Z-Move effects

When a Pokémon is holding Psychium Z and uses its Z-Power, Magic Room turns into Z-Magic Room and raises Special Defense one stage, in addition to its usual effect as above.
