Phantom Force (move)

  • Contents
  • Flavor text

Move data

Type Ghost
Category   Physical
Power 90
Accuracy 100
PP 10  (max. 16)
Makes contact? Yes
Introduced Generation 6

Machine/Record

Sword​/​Shield TM86

Effects

Phantom Force causes the user to disappear on the first turn, becoming invulnerable to most attacks. On the second turn, it deals damage and can hit Pokémon using Protect or Detect.

Changes

  • In Generation 6 if the target has used Minimize, Phantom Force deals double damage and ignores accuracy.

Z-Move effects

When a Pokémon is holding Ghostium Z and uses its Z-Power, Phantom Force turns into Never-Ending Nightmare and has base power 175.
