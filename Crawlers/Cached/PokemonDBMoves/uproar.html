Uproar (move)

  • Contents
  • Flavor text
  • Learnt by level up
  • Learnt by TR

Move data

Type Normal
Category   Special
Power 90
Accuracy 100
PP 10  (max. 16)
Makes contact? No
Introduced Generation 3

Machine/Record

Sword​/​Shield TR35

Effects

Uproar inflicts damage for 3 turns. During this time, no Pokémon on the field will be able to sleep, and any sleeping Pokémon will be woken up.

Changes

  • In Generation 3, Uproar is categorized as Physical.
  • In Generations 3-4, Uproar has 50 base power.
  • In Generations 3-4, the attack lasts 2-5 turns.

Z-Move effects

When a Pokémon is holding Normalium Z and uses its Z-Power, Uproar turns into Breakneck Blitz and has base power 175.
