Uproar (move)

Move data

Type Normal
Category   Special
Power 90
Accuracy 100
PP 10  (max. 16)
Introduced Generation 3

Effects

Uproar inflicts damage for 3 turns. During this time, no Pokémon on the field will be able to sleep, and any sleeping Pokémon will be woken up.

Changes

In Generations 1-4, Uproar has a base power of 50 and lasts 2-5 turns.

Z-Move effects

When a Pokémon is holding Normalium Z and uses its Z-Power, Uproar turns into Breakneck Blitz and has base power 175.
