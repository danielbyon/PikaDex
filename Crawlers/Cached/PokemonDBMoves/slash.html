Move data

Type Normal
Category   Physical
Power 70
Accuracy 100
PP 20  (max. 32)
Makes contact? Yes
Introduced Generation 1

Effects

Slash deals damage and has an increased critical hit ratio (1⁄8 instead of 1⁄24).

Z-Move effects

When a Pokémon is holding Normalium Z and uses its Z-Power, Slash turns into Breakneck Blitz and has base power 140.
