Move data

Type Ground
Category   Special
Power 55
Accuracy 95
PP 15  (max. 24)
Makes contact? No
Introduced Generation 3

Machine/Record

Sword​/​Shield TM53
Scarlet​/​Violet TM05

Effects

Mud Shot deals damage and lowers the target's Speed by one stage.

Stats can be lowered to a minimum of -6 stages each.

Changes

  • In Generation 3, Mud Shot is categorized as Physical.

Z-Move effects

When a Pokémon is holding Groundium Z and uses its Z-Power, Mud Shot turns into Tectonic Rage and has base power 100.
