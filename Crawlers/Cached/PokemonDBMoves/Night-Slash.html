Night Slash (move)

  • Contents
  • Flavor text

Move data

Type Dark
Category   Physical
Power 70
Accuracy 100
PP 15  (max. 24)
Makes contact? Yes
Introduced Generation 4

Effects

Night Slash deals damage and has an increased critical hit ratio (1⁄8 instead of 1⁄24).

Z-Move effects

When a Pokémon is holding Darkinium Z and uses its Z-Power, Night Slash turns into Black Hole Eclipse and has base power 140.
