Tera Blast (move)

This page is about a new move; details may be incomplete.

  • Contents
  • Flavor text
  • Learnt by TM

Move data

Type Normal
Category   Special
Power 80
Accuracy 100
PP 10  (max. 16)
Makes contact? No
Introduced Generation 9

Machine/Record

Scarlet​/​Violet TM171

Effects

Tera Blast deals damage, and its type changes to the user's Tera Type, when the Pokémon is Terastallized.

Tera Blast uses the Pokémon's Attack or Special Attack stat, whichever is higher.

Move target

Currently unknown.

Game descriptions

Scarlet​/​Violet If the user has Terastallized, it unleashes energy of its Tera Type. This move inflicts damage using the Attack or Sp. Atk stat—whichever is higher for the user.
