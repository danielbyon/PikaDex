Move data

Type Water
Category   Special
Power 40
Accuracy 100
PP 25  (max. 40)
Makes contact? No
Introduced Generation 1

Machine/Record

Red​/​Blue
Yellow		 TM12

Effects

Water Gun deals damage with no additional effect.

Z-Move effects

When a Pokémon is holding Waterium Z and uses its Z-Power, Water Gun turns into Hydro Vortex and has base power 100.
