Crunch (move)

  • Contents
  • Flavor text
  • Learnt by level up

Move data

Type Dark
Category   Physical
Power 80
Accuracy 100
PP 15  (max. 24)
Makes contact? Yes
Introduced Generation 2

Machine/Record

Sword​/​Shield TR32

Effects

Crunch deals damage and has a 20% chance of lowering the target's Defense by one stage.

Stats can be lowered to a minimum of -6 stages each.

Changes

  • In Generations 2-3, Crunch is categorized as Special.
  • In Generations 2-3, Crunch instead may lower Special Defense one stage. This is likely due to its Special categorization.

Z-Move effects

When a Pokémon is holding Darkinium Z and uses its Z-Power, Crunch turns into Black Hole Eclipse and has base power 160.
