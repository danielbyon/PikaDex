Move data

Category   Physical
Power 50
Accuracy 100
PP 20  (max. 32)
Makes contact? No
Introduced Generation 9

Machine/Record

Scarlet​/​Violet TM20

Effects

Trailblaze deals damage and raises the user's speed by one stage.

Scarlet​/​Violet The user attacks suddenly as if leaping out from tall grass. The user’s nimble footwork boosts its Speed stat.
