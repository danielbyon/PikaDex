Rapid Spin (move)

  • Contents
  • Flavor text
  • Learnt by level up
  • Learnt by breeding

Move data

Type Normal
Category   Physical
Power 20
Accuracy 100
PP 40  (max. 64)
Introduced Generation 2

Effects

Rapid Spin deals a small amount of damage and removes the effects of several trap moves. It fails if the move misses or the target is immune. The effects from the following moves are removed:

  • Bind
  • Clamp
  • Fire Spin
  • Leech Seed
  • Magma Storm
  • Sand Tomb
  • Spikes
  • Stealth Rock
  • Toxic Spikes
  • Whirlpool
  • Wrap

Z-Move effects

When a Pokémon is holding Normalium Z and uses its Z-Power, Rapid Spin turns into Breakneck Blitz and has base power 100.
