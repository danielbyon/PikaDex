Effects

Shadow Claw deals damage and has an increased critical hit ratio (1⁄8 instead of 1⁄24).

Z-Move effects

When a Pokémon is holding Ghostium Z and uses its Z-Power, Shadow Claw turns into Never-Ending Nightmare and has base power 140.
