Move data

Type Fighting
Category   Physical
Power 40
Accuracy 100
PP 20  (max. 32)
Makes contact? Yes
Introduced Generation 6

Machine/Record

X​/​Y
O.Ruby​/​A.Sapphire		 TM98

Effects

Power-Up Punch deals damage and raises the user's Attack by one stage.

Stats can be raised to a maximum of +6 stages each.

Z-Move effects

When a Pokémon is holding Fightinium Z and uses its Z-Power, Power-Up Punch turns into All-Out Pummeling and has base power 100.
