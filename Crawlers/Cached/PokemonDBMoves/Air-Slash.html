Move data

Type Flying
Category   Special
Power 75
Accuracy 95
PP 15  (max. 24)
Makes contact? No
Introduced Generation 4

Machine/Record

Sword​/​Shield TM95
Scarlet​/​Violet TM65

Effects

Air Slash deals damage and has a 30% chance of causing the target to flinch (if the target has not yet moved).

Pokémon with the ability Inner Focus or those behind a Substitute cannot be made to flinch.

Changes

  • In Generations 4-5, Air Slash has 20 PP.

Z-Move effects

When a Pokémon is holding Flyinium Z and uses its Z-Power, Air Slash turns into Supersonic Skystrike and has base power 140.
