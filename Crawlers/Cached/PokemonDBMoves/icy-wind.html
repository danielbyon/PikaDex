Move data

Type Ice

Technical/Hidden Machine

Gold​/​Silver
Crystal		 TM16
Sword​/​Shield TM27

Effects

Icy Wind deals damage and lowers the target's Speed by one stage.

Stats can be lowered to a minimum of -6 stages each.

Z-Move effects

When a Pokémon is holding Icium Z and uses its Z-Power, Icy Wind turns into Subzero Slammer and has base power 100.
