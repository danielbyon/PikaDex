Move data

Type Flying
Category   Physical
Power 35
Accuracy 100
PP 35  (max. 56)
Introduced Generation 1

Effects

Peck deals damage with no additional effect, besides being able to hit non-adjacent Pokémon in triple battles.

Z-Move effects

When a Pokémon is holding Flyinium Z and uses its Z-Power, Peck turns into Supersonic Skystrike and has base power 100.

Move target

Foe
Foe

User

Ally

Targets any single Pokémon on the field including non-adjacent ones.
