Move data

Type Normal
Category   Physical
Power 85
Accuracy 100
PP 15  (max. 24)
Makes contact? Yes
Introduced Generation 1

Machine/Record

Red​/​Blue
Yellow		 TM08
Sword​/​Shield TR01
Scarlet​/​Violet TM66

Effects

Body Slam deals damage and has a 30% chance of paralyzing the target.

Electric type Pokémon, those with the ability Limber or those behind a Substitute cannot be paralyzed.

If the target has used Minimize, Body Slam ignores accuracy and deals double damage.

Changes

  • In Generations 1-5, the Minimize effect did not occur.

Z-Move effects

When a Pokémon is holding Normalium Z and uses its Z-Power, Body Slam turns into Breakneck Blitz and has base power 160.
