Assurance (move)

  • Contents
  • Flavor text
  • Learnt by level up

Move data

Type Dark
Category   Physical
Power 60
Accuracy 100
PP 10  (max. 16)
Makes contact? Yes
Introduced Generation 4

Machine/Record

Sword​/​Shield TM58

Effects

Assurance deals damage, and if the target has already taken damage in this turn its power doubles to 120.

Changes

  • In Generations 4-5, Assurance has 50 base power.

Z-Move effects

When a Pokémon is holding Darkinium Z and uses its Z-Power, Assurance turns into Black Hole Eclipse and has base power 120.
