Feint Attack (move)

  • Contents
  • Flavor text
  • Learnt by level up
  • Learnt by breeding

Move data

Type Dark
Category   Physical
Power 60
Accuracy ∞
PP 20  (max. 32)
Makes contact? Yes
Introduced Generation 2

Effects

Feint Attack deals damage and ignores changes to the Accuracy and Evasion stats. However, it will not hit Pokémon during the invulnerable stage of Bounce, Dig, Dive, Fly, Shadow Force or Sky Drop.

Changes

  • In Generations 2-3, Feint Attack is categorized as Special.
  • In Generations 2-5, this move's name is formatted as Faint Attack.

Z-Move effects

When a Pokémon is holding Darkinium Z and uses its Z-Power, Feint Attack turns into Black Hole Eclipse and has base power 120.
