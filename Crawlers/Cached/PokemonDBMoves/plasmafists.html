Plasma Fists (move)

  • Contents
  • Flavor text

Move data

Type Electric
Category   Physical
Power 100
Accuracy 100
PP 15  (max. 24)
Introduced Generation 7

Effects

Changes Normal-type moves to Electric-type moves.

Z-Move effects

When a Pokémon is holding Electrium Z and uses its Z-Power, Plasma Fists turns into Gigavolt Havoc and has base power 180.

Ultra Sun​/​Ultra Moon The user attacks with electrically charged fists. This move changes Normal-type moves to Electric-type moves.
