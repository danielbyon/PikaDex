Gunk Shot (move)

  • Contents
  • Flavor text
  • Learnt by level up

Move data

Type Poison
Category   Physical
Power 120
Accuracy 80
PP 5  (max. 8)
Makes contact? No
Introduced Generation 4

Machine/Record

Sword​/​Shield TR73

Effects

Gunk Shot deals damage and has a 30% chance of poisoning the target.

Poison or Steel type Pokémon, those with the ability Immunity or those behind a Substitute cannot be poisoned.

Changes

  • In Generations 4-5, Gunk Shot has 70% accuracy.

Z-Move effects

When a Pokémon is holding Poisonium Z and uses its Z-Power, Gunk Shot turns into Acid Downpour and has base power 190.
