Astonish (move)

  • Contents
  • Flavor text
  • Learnt by level up
  • Learnt by breeding

Move data

Type Ghost
Category   Physical
Power 30
Accuracy 100
PP 15  (max. 24)
Makes contact? Yes
Introduced Generation 3

Effects

Astonish deals damage and has a 30% chance of causing the target to flinch (if the target has not yet moved).

Pokémon with the ability Inner Focus or those behind a Substitute cannot be made to flinch.

Changes

  • In Generation 3 if the target has used Minimize, Astonish deals double damage.

Z-Move effects

When a Pokémon is holding Ghostium Z and uses its Z-Power, Astonish turns into Never-Ending Nightmare and has base power 100.
