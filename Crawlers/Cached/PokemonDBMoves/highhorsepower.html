Move data

Type Ground
Category   Physical
Power 95
Accuracy 95
PP 10  (max. 16)
Makes contact? Yes
Introduced Generation 7

Machine/Record

Sword​/​Shield TR94

Effects

The user fiercely attacks the target using its entire body.

Z-Move effects

When a Pokémon is holding Groundium Z and uses its Z-Power, High Horsepower turns into Tectonic Rage and has base power 175.
