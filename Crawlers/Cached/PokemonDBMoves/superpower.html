Move data

Type Fighting
Category   Physical
Power 120
Accuracy 100
PP 5  (max. 8)
Makes contact? Yes
Introduced Generation 3

Machine/Record

L.G. Pikachu​/​L.G. Eevee TM49
Sword​/​Shield TR39

Effects

Superpower deals damage but lowers the user's Attack and Defense by one stage each after attacking.

Stats can be lowered to a minimum of -6 stages each.

Z-Move effects

When a Pokémon is holding Fightinium Z and uses its Z-Power, Superpower turns into All-Out Pummeling and has base power 190.
