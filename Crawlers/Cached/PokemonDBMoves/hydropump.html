Move data

Type Water
Category   Special
Power 110
Accuracy 80
PP 5  (max. 8)
Makes contact? No
Introduced Generation 1

Machine/Record

Sword​/​Shield TR03

Effects

Hydro Pump deals damage with no additional effect.

Changes

  • In Generations 1-5, Hydro Pump has 120 base power.

Z-Move effects

When a Pokémon is holding Waterium Z and uses its Z-Power, Hydro Pump turns into Hydro Vortex and has base power 185.
