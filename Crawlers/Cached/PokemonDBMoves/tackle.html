Move data

Type Normal
Category   Physical
Power 40
Accuracy 100
PP 35  (max. 56)
Introduced Generation 1

Effects

Tackle is one of the most common and basic moves a Pokémon learns. It deals damage with no additional effects.

Changes

In Generations 1-4, Tackle has a base power of 35 and 95% accuracy.

In Generations 5-6, Tackle has a base power of 50.

Z-Move effects

When a Pokémon is holding Normalium Z and uses its Z-Power, Tackle turns into Breakneck Blitz and has base power 100.
