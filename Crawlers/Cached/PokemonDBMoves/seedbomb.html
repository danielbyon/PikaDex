Move data

Type Grass
Category   Physical
Power 80
Accuracy 100
PP 15  (max. 24)
Makes contact? No
Introduced Generation 4

Machine/Record

Sword​/​Shield TR59

Effects

Seed Bomb deals damage with no additional effect.

Z-Move effects

When a Pokémon is holding Grassium Z and uses its Z-Power, Seed Bomb turns into Bloom Doom and has base power 160.
