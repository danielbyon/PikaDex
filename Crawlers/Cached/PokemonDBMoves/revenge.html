Move data

Type Fighting
Category   Physical
Power 60
Accuracy 100
PP 10  (max. 16)
Priority -4
Makes contact? Yes
Introduced Generation 3

Machine/Record

Sword​/​Shield TM42

Effects

Power increases if user was hit first.

Z-Move effects

When a Pokémon is holding Fightinium Z and uses its Z-Power, Revenge turns into All-Out Pummeling and has base power 120.
