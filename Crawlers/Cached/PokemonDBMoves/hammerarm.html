Hammer Arm (move)

  • Contents
  • Flavor text
  • Learnt by level up
  • Learnt by breeding

Move data

Type Fighting
Category   Physical
Power 100
Accuracy 90
PP 10  (max. 16)
Makes contact? Yes
Introduced Generation 4

Effects

Hammer Arm deals damage but lowers the user's Speed by one stage after attacking.

Stats can be lowered to a minimum of -6 stages each.

Z-Move effects

When a Pokémon is holding Fightinium Z and uses its Z-Power, Hammer Arm turns into All-Out Pummeling and has base power 180.
