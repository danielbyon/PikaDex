Ancient Power (move)

  • Contents
  • Flavor text
  • Learnt by level up
  • Learnt by breeding

Move data

Type Rock
Category   Special
Power 60
Accuracy 100
PP 5  (max. 8)
Makes contact? No
Introduced Generation 2

Effects

Ancient Power deals damage and has a 10% chance of raising all the user's stats at once - Attack, Defense, Special Attack, Special Defense and Speed - by one stage each.

Stats can be raised to a maximum of +6 stages each.

Evolution

The following Pokémon evolve after learning Ancient Power:

  • Tangela (evolves into Tangrowth) - learnt at level 38 from Pokémon X/Y onwards (level 33, 36 or 40 in earlier games).
  • Yanma (evolves into Yanmega) - learnt at level 33.
  • Piloswine (evolves into Mamoswine) - taught via the Move Reminder or by breeding.

Changes

  • In Generations 2-3, Ancient Power is categorized as Physical.
  • In Generations 2-5, this move's name is formatted as AncientPower.

Z-Move effects

When a Pokémon is holding Rockium Z and uses its Z-Power, Ancient Power turns into Continental Crush and has base power 120.
