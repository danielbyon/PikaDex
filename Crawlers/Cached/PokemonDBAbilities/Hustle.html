Hustle (ability)

Effect

Hustle increases the ability-bearer's Attack by 50%, however, it also reduces the Accuracy of all Physical moves by 20%.

Outside of battle

If a Pokémon with Hustle is the leading party Pokémon, wild Pokémon are 50% more likely to be higher level.
