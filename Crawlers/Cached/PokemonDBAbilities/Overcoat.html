Overcoat (ability)

Effect

Overcoat makes the Pokémon immune to residual damage from weather conditions (specifically Hail and Sandstorm).

Game descriptions

Black​/​White
Black 2​/​White 2		 Protects the Pokémon from damage from weather.
X​/​Y
O.Ruby​/​A.Sapphire		 Protects the Pokémon from things like sand, hail, and powder.
Sun​/​Moon
Ultra Sun​/​Ultra Moon		 Protects the Pokémon from things like sand, hail, and powder.

B.Diamond​/​S.Pearl		 Protects the Pokémon from things like sand, hail, and powder.
Scarlet​/​Violet The Pokémon takes no damage from sandstorms. It is also protected from the effects of powders and spores.
