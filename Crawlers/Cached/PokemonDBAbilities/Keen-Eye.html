Keen Eye (ability)

Effect

Keen Eye prevents the Pokémon from losing accuracy, for example from moves like Sand-Attack.

Outside of battle

If a Pokémon with Keen Eye is the leading party Pokémon, wild Pokémon are 50% less likely to be lower level.
