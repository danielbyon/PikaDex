Summary

This is the Pokémon Global Link artwork of #385 Jirachi, released in Pokémon Black and White.
This artwork of Jirachi is used as a badge in Nintendo Badge Arcade.
This artwork of Jirachi is used as a Spirit in Super Smash Bros. Ultimate.
