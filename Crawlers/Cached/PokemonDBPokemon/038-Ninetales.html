Ninetales changes

  • In Generation 1, Ninetales has a base Special stat of 100.
  • In Generations 3-4, Ninetales has 1 Special Attack EV.
  • In Generations 3-4, Ninetales has 1 Special Defense EV.
  • In Generations 1-4, Ninetales has a base experience yield of 178.
  • In Generations 2-7, Ninetales has a base Friendship value of 70.
  • In Generation 7, Alolan Ninetales has a base Friendship value of 70.
