Mr. Mime is a Psychic/Fairy type Pokémon introduced in Generation 1. It is known as the Barrier Pokémon.

Mr. Mime has a Galarian form in Pokémon Sword & Shield, which evolves into Mr. Rime.

Mr. Mime changes

  • In Generations 1-5, Mr. Mime is Psychic type.
  • In Generation 3, Mr. Mime does not have the Filter ability.
  • In Generation 1, Mr. Mime has a base Special stat of 100.
  • In Generations 1-4, Mr. Mime has a base experience yield of 136.
  • In Generations 2-7, Mr. Mime has a base Friendship value of 70.
